Privacy Policy

ShopJob respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

Information We Collect

We may collect information provided when creating an account, posting jobs, communicating with service providers, verifying identity, or contacting support.

How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and improve our services
  • Verify user accounts
  • Facilitate communication between users
  • Send account-related notifications
  • Maintain platform security
  • Respond to support requests

SMS Communications

Users may provide a mobile phone number during account registration.

ShopJob may send SMS messages for:

  • Account verification codes
  • Verification confirmations
  • Account security notifications
  • Job and order updates
  • Service-related notifications

Message frequency varies.

Message and data rates may apply.

Users may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP.

Users may request assistance by replying HELP.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.
