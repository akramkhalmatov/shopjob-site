SMS Verification and Notifications

Users may register for ShopJob using their mobile phone number.

By creating an account and providing a mobile phone number, users consent to receive SMS messages from ShopJob for account-related purposes.

ShopJob may send SMS messages for:

  • Account verification codes
  • Verification confirmations
  • Service request notifications
  • Job and order updates
  • Account security notifications
  • Other essential account-related communications

Message frequency varies.

Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to opt out.

Reply HELP for assistance.

Marketing or promotional SMS messages are not sent without separate user consent.
